Organ System Survival

An organ is falling toward the body. Tap the correct organ system card — the one whose organs include the falling organ.

🎮 How to Play

  • An organ (e.g., "🫘 Kidney") drops from the top.
  • All 8 organ systems are shown at the bottom as cards, each previewing its organs.
  • Tap the card whose organ list matches the falling organ before it reaches the body.
  • Correct = +points, combo increases.
  • Wrong or missed = homeostasis drops.
  • Reach 0% → game over. Keep the body alive as long as you can!
  • ⚠️ Every wave is faster — speed increases by +35 px/s each wave.
  • 💡 Keyboard: Press 1–8 to pick a card. Space to pause.
